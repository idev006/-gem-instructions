### Round-1 defects

#### D1 — Ruler mapping over-specialized
The ruler engine used a shortcut tied to 1 mm minor divisions. This could become wrong when another minor division is configured.
Severity: MAJOR / potentially CRITICAL_ACADEMIC.
Repair: generalized tick mapping to `(value - scale_min) / minor_division`; added start/end tick metadata and representability checks.

#### D2 — Thermometer physical mapping incomplete
The engine calculated a tick index but did not explicitly lock vertical direction, normalized physical height, zero handling across negative/positive ranges, or scale reversal prevention.
Severity: CRITICAL_ACADEMIC for negative/mixed scales.
Repair: added bottom=min/top=max rule, `level_ratio`, zero/negative-label requirements, scale-direction QA.

#### D3 — Capacity engine assumed zero baseline
`tick_index = target/minor_division` was unsafe for a nonzero displayed baseline.
Severity: MAJOR / potentially CRITICAL_ACADEMIC.
Repair: added `SCALE_MIN`, general mapping, `level_ratio`, scale-direction rules and meniscus convention lock.

#### D4 — Maturity governance inconsistent with evidence
Scale reading was labeled `PRODUCTION_HARDENED` although actual rendered examples had exposed systemic geometry failures and the promotion policy requires >=10 clean audited renders.
Severity: PROCESS CRITICAL / false assurance risk.
Repair: registry and release matrix demoted `MEASUREMENT_WEIGHT` to `PRODUCTION_CANDIDATE` until evidence threshold is satisfied.

## Case results after repair

### TIME
PASS. Whole-hour and mixed-minute calculations are deterministic. Midnight/zero-duration guards and answer leakage separation are coherent.

### SCALE READING
PASS at rule/dry-run level. 300° active sweep avoids 0/5 overlap; center pivot, one needle, 0.1 kg ticks, exact target angle, square dial zone, and 2x5 layout policy are coherent.
Status remains CANDIDATE because actual-render evidence is insufficient.

### CLOCK
PASS dry-run. 7:30 correctly places hour hand halfway between 7 and 8. 9:45 continuously interpolates hour hand. Candidate pending render evidence.

### LENGTH
PASS after repair. Arbitrary minor divisions now map correctly; zero graduation is distinct from ruler edge; nonzero-start task uses end-start.

### TEMPERATURE
PASS after repair. Negative/positive range, zero mark, scale direction and physical column level are deterministic.

### CAPACITY
PASS after repair. Nonzero baseline and target level mapping no longer assume zero; meniscus convention is explicit.

### MONEY
PASS dry-run for totals/change and price-label association. Denomination-recognition worksheets still require verified denomination data and render review.
